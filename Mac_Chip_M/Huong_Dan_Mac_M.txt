   AI KARAOKE STUDIO PRO - HUONG DAN SU DUNG CHO MAC CHIP M (APPLE SILICON)
   (Ap dung cho tat ca MacBook Air, Pro, Mac mini, iMac, Mac Studio chip M1/M2/M3/M4)
=======================================================================

1. XU LY QUYEN TRUOC KHI CHAY (Neu moi tai file ZIP ve tu Internet/Drive/Zalo):
   - Khi tai file tu Internet ve, tinh nang Gatekeeper cua macOS co the chan file .command.
   - Ban chi can lam 1 trong 2 cach sau (chi can lam 1 lan dau tien):
     + Cach 1 (Nhanh nhat): Mo ung dung Terminal tren Mac (Cmd + Space, go Terminal).
       Keo file "MO_KHOA_QUYEN_MAC.command" tha vao cua so Terminal roi an Enter.
     + Cach 2: Mo Terminal, go lenh:
       chmod +x Mac_Chip_M/*.command && xattr -cr .


2. CAI DAT MOI TRUONG (Chi chay 1 lan dau tien):
   - Nhap dup vao file:  1_Cai_Dat_Mac_M.command
   - He thong tu dong tao moi truong ao "venv_mac" rieng biet va tai thu vien AI.
   - May Mac can co san Python 3 (khuyen nghi 3.10 hoac 3.11). Neu may chua co,
     script se tu dong mo trang tai ban cai dat chuan cho macOS.
   - Mac chip M se tu dong su dung bo ma hoa video phan cung Apple VideoToolbox
     (Apple Media Engine) giup xuat video Full HD cuc nhanh ma may van mat me.


3. KHOI DONG PHONG THU:
   - Nhap dup vao file:  2_Khoi_Dong_Mac_M.command
   - Trinh duyet Safari / Chrome se tu dong mo dia chi: http://127.0.0.1:8008
   - Phong thu AI san sang hoat dong 100% offline tren may cua ban!


4. TOI UU KHI TACH BEAT & NHAN DIEN LOI (DEMUCS & WHISPER):
   - Demucs chay toi uu hoa tren da luong CPU ARM NEON va bo nho hop nhat
     (Unified Memory) cua chip Apple Silicon, dam bao on dinh 100% khong bi loi.
   - Whisper nhan dien loi tieng Viet cuc nhanh voi thu vien Apple Accelerate (~3 giay).

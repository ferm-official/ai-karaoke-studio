=======================================================================
   AI KARAOKE STUDIO PRO - HUONG DAN SU DUNG CHO MACOS INTEL / CHUNG
   (Ap dung cho cac may Mac su dung vi xu ly Intel Core i5/i7/i9)
=======================================================================

1. CAI DAT MOI TRUONG (Chi chay 1 lan dau tien):
   - Nhap dup vao file:  1_Cai_Dat_MacOS_Intel.command
   - He thong se tu dong thiet lap venv va cai dat thu vien AI.
   - Mac Intel se su dung che do tinh toan CPU da luong ket hop bo ma
     hoa video Apple VideoToolbox ho tro boi Intel QuickSync.

2. KHOI DONG PHONG THU:
   - Nhap dup vao file:  2_Khoi_Dong_MacOS_Intel.command
   - He thong se tu dong mo trinh duyet Safari / Chrome tai: http://127.0.0.1:8008

3. XU LY NEU MAC BAO KHONG CO QUYEN CHAY FILE .COMMAND:
   - Khi tai ve tu Internet, macOS co the chan file .command.
   - Cach mo quyen cuc ky don gian:
     1) Mo ung dung Terminal tren Mac (nhan Cmd + Space, go Terminal).
     2) Go lenh sau roi an Enter:
        chmod +x *.command

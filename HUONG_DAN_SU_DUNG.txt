               AI KARAOKE STUDIO PRO - HUONG DAN SU DUNG
=======================================================================

Phan mem ho tro day du 3 he thong voi cac thu muc rieng biet:

1. THU MUC: Windows/ (Danh cho may tinh Windows 10, 11)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cai dat moi truong:
  Mo folder "Windows" va nhap dup vao:  1_Cai_Dat_Windows.bat
  He thong tu dong tao venv, tai thu vien AI va kich hoat NVIDIA CUDA.
- Khoi dong phong thu:
  Mo folder "Windows" va nhap dup vao:  2_Khoi_Dong_Windows.bat
  Trinh duyet se tu dong mo dia chi: http://127.0.0.1:8008


2. THU MUC: Mac_Chip_M/ (Danh cho Mac Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cai dat moi truong:
  Mo folder "Mac_Chip_M" va nhap dup vao:  1_Cai_Dat_Mac_M.command
  He thong tu dong thiet lap tang toc GPU Metal (MPS) va Apple VideoToolbox.
- Khoi dong phong thu:
  Mo folder "Mac_Chip_M" va nhap dup vao:  2_Khoi_Dong_Mac_M.command
  Trinh duyet se tu dong mo: http://127.0.0.1:8008
- Neu macOS thong bao chua co quyen chay file:
  Mo Terminal go lenh: chmod +x Mac_Chip_M/*.command


3. THU MUC: MacOS_Intel/ (Danh cho Mac dung CPU Intel Core i5/i7/i9)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cai dat moi truong:
  Mo folder "MacOS_Intel" va nhap dup vao:  1_Cai_Dat_MacOS_Intel.command
- Khoi dong phong thu:
  Mo folder "MacOS_Intel" va nhap dup vao:  2_Khoi_Dong_MacOS_Intel.command
- Neu macOS thong bao chua co quyen chay file:
  Mo Terminal go lenh: chmod +x MacOS_Intel/*.command


4. QUY TRINH TAO VIDEO KARAOKE:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Buoc 1: Dan link YouTube hoac tai len file am thanh (MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A).
- Buoc 2: Nhan "Bat dau tao Karaoke" de AI tach beat va nhan dien loi.
- Buoc 3: Vao tab "Phong Thu" de tinh chinh loi, keo chuot truc tiep tren man hinh
          de chinh vi tri Dong 1 / Dong 2, chon kieu dem nhip (Trai Tim / Mat Cuoi).
- Buoc 4: Nhan "Xuat Video MP4 Full HD" hoac tai file phu de ASS / LRC.
